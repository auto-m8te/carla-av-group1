# Group 1 — Autonomous vehicle in CARLA

Engineering Teamwork, WS2026/27, SRH Berlin (A. Laboudi).
Team: Khalid · Abir · Adil · Prithiv · Omar.

A simulated car in CARLA 0.9.16 that follows its lane, detects obstacles with LiDAR,
and either stops or steers around them.
