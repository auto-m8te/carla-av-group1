Original 800x450 BGR camera PNGs copied losslessly from the tune split
(`drive 0`) of `data/lane`, with source records in `labels.json`.
These three examples cover a curb/yellow-line street, an urban multilane road,
and a highway. They are regression fixtures, not an evaluation sample.
The synthetic fixtures use parallel boundaries converging at the calibrated
camera horizon; translating the entire image would also introduce heading.
